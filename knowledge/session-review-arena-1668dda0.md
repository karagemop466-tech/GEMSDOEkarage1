### 3.1 Exact Set-Algebraic Collar-Ablation Ladder on the Mirrored Footprint
Let $D_0 \subset F$ denote the binary dot support of `GEMSDOE25` (`5968b382...`), which is pixel-for-pixel identical on the `5,167,373`-pixel active footprint $F$ to `GEMSDOE30` (`7810e9fb...`), with $|D_0| = 44{,}090$ (`d2.8` Poisson-disk spacing $\ge 2.8\text{ px} = 280\text{ m}$ over a topographic-scarp/potential-field detector). ### 3.2 Pooled Distance-Weighted Tversky Calculus ($\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8, R = 300\text{ m}$)
Under the [official competition metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric), with kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d / 3\text{ px}, 0)$, hidden unmapped fault pixels $G \subset F \setminus C$, weighted recall sum $T = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{x \in P} p(x)\,k(\|x - g\|_2)$, weighted false-positive mass $F_p = \sum_{x \in P} p(x)\,[1 - \max_{g \in G} k(\|x - g\|_2)]$, and $D = T + \alpha F_p + \beta(|G| - T) = 0.2(T + F_p) + 0.8|G|$:

1. **Why $r \le 2\text{ px}$ (`200 m`) deletion can raise DTI (`0.2600 -> 0.2708 -> 0.2778` user-reported):**
   Per [DrivenData staff thread 11516](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516), **only exact catalogue pixels $c \in C$ are masked**; pixels at $1 \le d(x, C) \le 2\text{ px}$ (`100–200 m`) remain active in $F \setminus C$. However, topographic-slope/curvature detectors fire heavily along the **digitization/scarp-width halo** of already-mapped range-front faults in $C$, where map-registration offset ($\sim 50\text{–}200\text{ m}$) creates strong scarp gradients without an independent unmapped strand $g \in G$. Deleting a collar subset $\Delta P$ that loses weighted truth coverage $\Delta T = L \ge 0$ while shedding false-positive mass $\Delta F_p = c > 0$ strictly increases DTI iff:
   $$\alpha T c > L\bigl(\alpha F_p + \beta |G|\bigr) \iff \frac{L}{c} < \frac{0.2\,\text{DTI}}{1 - 0.2\,\text{DTI}}$$
   At $\text{DTI} \approx 0.27$, any collar dot whose marginal truth-to-FP yield $L/c < 0.057$ is net-destructive. Notably, across every newly listed peer family (`ApartGEMSDOE1` `0.2747`, `GINNYGEMSDOE1` `0.2718`, `WBGGEMSDOE1` `0.2709`, `GEMSDOE92` `0.2699`, `KANLERGEMSDOE3` `0.2673`), **every user-reported variant built on the `d2.8` + collar/tip-cone template plateaued inside `[0.267, 0.278]`**, never reaching `0.3195` or `0.4413`.

2. **Why `0.2778` is a Structural Ceiling for Sparse-Dot Collar Pruning — and What Separates `0.3195` / `0.4413`:**
   - **Recall-dominated asymmetry ($\beta = 0.8 = 4\alpha$):** Because missed fault pixels carry $4\times$ the denominator weight of false-positive mass, sparse point lattices (`d2.8` spacing $\approx 2.8\text{ px}$) leave scalloped kernel troughs ($k(1.4\text{ px}) = 0.533$) even along perfectly detected fault traces, capping average per-pixel kernel weight along recovered traces well below $1.0$ unless continuous or calibrated soft/ridge support covers the trace.
   - **Why naive sparse/unique dot cuts fail (`GEMSDOE91` `S18-UNIQUE`: `20,000` dots $\to$ `0.0435`; `BB-STEP`: `17,766` dots $\to$ `HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0318`):** Cutting the emission budget or firing on single-modality ridges without calibrated trace continuity starves $T$ against the fixed $0.8|G|$ denominator.
