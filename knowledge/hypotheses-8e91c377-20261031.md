## The metric fact that drives the ranking (vendor-verified)

`vendor/gems57/metric.py` (provenance-pinned): DTI = TP / (TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·FN), triangular kernel radius 3 px (300 m).

Because 1 − β = α = 0.2, the denominator is 0.2·(TP+FP) + 0.8·n, where n is the number of truth pixels. A dot of marginal credit k raises DTI only if **k > 0.2 · DTI**. At DTI ≈ 0.28 that threshold is k ≈ 0.056, i.e. the dot's nearest truth must lie within about 2.8 px. This is an algebraic consequence of the vendored formula, not an empirical finding.
