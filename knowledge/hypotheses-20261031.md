## Candidate selection and next experiment

**No candidate is selected or promoted.** Rank 1 is the preferred *research question* only if an authorized lane and the canonical shared template are supplied; it is not a submission recommendation. The current branch's `registry/template.json` has both `method_lane` and `canonical_template_assignment` set to `null`. The shared feature cache and competition rasters are missing. Two vendored shared-tool modules fail their recorded SHA-256 pins. The mandated rule therefore forbids running the candidate through a private substitute.

Once the blockers are resolved, the first experiment must:

1. freeze the exact CP-DPCS definition and all thresholds before accessing withheld segment labels;
2. test every input feature alone as a leakage canary (AUC > 0.90 is an alarm pending investigation);
3. use the assigned shared holdout/evaluator, with complete fault segments withheld and a buffer, feature construction from visible faults only, and exact masking of visible faults;
4. compare pooled DTI (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) to the same-fold frozen baseline, report withheld-positive count and 95% CI, and disclose the known target mismatch; and
5. evaluate the continuous surface against every authorized registry raster before placement, then repeat rank-correlation and 3-px dot-overlap checks on the final dots. A detected duplicate is logged and terminates the experiment.

The evaluator and `submission_writer.py` must be the approved shared versions. Fix shared tooling in its owning template; do not patch a private fork. At most three experiments or two hours are allowed. No weekly slot may be used based on a proposal or holdout proxy alone.
