# Candidate hypotheses for the next authorized GEMS experiment

**Status: preregistration proposals only.** This slate was written before implementation. “Not tried” is scoped carefully: the precise tests below were not found in the reviewed active feature/experiment code in this checkout; the repository's previous hypothesis notes contain related but non-identical work. That is not proof of novelty across all other GEMSDOE sites, papers, or parallel participants. Do not call any candidate globally unique until the complete registry and surface tests are available.

Ranking is qualitative expected utility per cost, not a numerical expected DTI gain and not a score projection. “Higher” means the physical test appears more discriminating against the local implementation set; the holdout decides. All hypotheses require authenticated competition data and an assigned lane. No additional external dataset is required for the first two; the third depends on resolving a layer-metadata discrepancy.

## Candidate selection for future validation (not a submission selection)

If a lane owner assigns this topic and authenticates the inputs, **Rank 1** is the first validation candidate because the repo's positive-strain/seismicity-gradient feature gives a direct, testable contrast: strain-loaded low-seismicity corridors are a different physical proposition, not a cosmetic parameter change. Its chief risk is that the geodetic and earthquake layers may be smoothed, biased, or unrelated at the target scale. Rank 2 is a lower-risk transform of existing layers but is closer to current potential-field methods. Rank 3 is blocked by the source-depth band mismatch.

Before any placement or submission writer call, each feature must be tested alone on the authorized spatial holdout. AUC above 0.90 is a leakage alarm until resolved. For Rank 1, all catalogue-derived inputs (if any are used in comparison/control features) must be fold-local; visible faults are masked pixel-exactly in the score domain. Use whole-segment buffered hides, pooled DTI with alpha 0.2 / beta 0.8 / 300 m triangular kernel, and a paired CI against a frozen same-evaluator baseline. Test the continuous surface against every registry raster before placement; stop on `|Spearman| > 0.90`. After placement, stop if more than 70% of emitted dots are within 3 pixels of any registry raster. Budget remains no more than three experiments or two hours. A positive local holdout does not equal an organizer score or authorize a weekly submission slot.
