# Historical CDA1 Preregistration — Superseded and Quarantined

> **DO NOT USE AS A CURRENT LANE, RESULT, OR SUBMISSION AUTHORIZATION.** This file replaces an older CDA1 preregistration note whose score and validation statements were not reliable. `registry/template.json` currently has `method_lane: null`, so CDA1 is not the active assigned lane.

## What is retained

An earlier session proposed using drainage-network deflection and knickpoints as potential indicators of faults concealed beneath alluvium. Plausible mimics include Lake Lahontan shorelines, fan-terrace risers, nontectonic stream piracy, and anthropogenic drainage changes. This remains a qualitative research idea only; it is not a verified geothermal discovery, a validated fault predictor, or a numerical forecast.

The older CDA1 GeoTIFF in `archive/cda1-demonstrator/` is explicitly classified as a **synthetic demonstrator**, not a competition prediction. [`docs/data/archive-review.json`](../docs/data/archive-review.json) records that its archived `spatially_blocked_holdout` returned hard-coded numbers independent of the candidate input and that the TIFF is a literal duplicate of the pinned lattice under the 3-pixel rule. Consequently, historical CDA1 numbers, confidence intervals, canary claims, and claimed relative improvements are **not valid HOLDOUT-DTI evidence** and are omitted from current score tables.

The prior document also asserted a 439-raster complete registry and described several external layers as present or verified. Those claims were not independently established. The current repository records the registry as incomplete and distinguishes owner-mirrored data from organizer-authenticated data.
