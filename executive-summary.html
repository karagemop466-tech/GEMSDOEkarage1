FOR A FUTURE CLEARED CANDIDATE

How to submit, once eligible.

These steps are not permission to upload either historical artifact.

01 / EVIDENCE

Pass the automated gates

Use the shared evaluator, fold-local features and per-feature canaries. Beat a frozen comparable holdout best; audit surface and dots against the complete registry; reopen the TIFF and validate saved bytes.

02 / SELECTOR

Choose a slot separately

Only an independent selector may choose an eligible candidate and check the current allowance on the authenticated submission page. The September rules say three submissions per week.

03 / DOWNLOAD

Choose the exact file

Download the approved single-band GeoTIFF (or a ZIP containing one GeoTIFF). Check its displayed SHA-256, unique name and note of at most 140 characters.

04 / UPLOAD

Use the official form

Open DrivenData, select Submit → Make new submission, choose the .tif or ZIP, and paste the note. Retain the file-linked organizer receipt.
