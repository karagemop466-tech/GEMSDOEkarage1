WHY GEMSDOE32 B2 REPORTED 0.2778

PhD-level reading

The number is not verified. gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif reported 0.2778 by the site owner, but no artifact-linked organizer submission receipt is in this repository, so it cannot be treated as ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED or used as a score target.

Mechanism (geometric, not causal). On the audited mirrored grid the B2 mask is exactly the GEMSDOE25 d2.8 dot mask with 6,436 dots within two pixels of the visible catalogue removed and no dots added. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 share an identical dot mask. Under the organizer's distance-weighted Tversky index (α = 0.2, β = 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel; known-catalogue pixels masked before scoring), thinning removes false-positive mass around known faults while largely keeping distance-weighted coverage of nearby truth cells. That can raise DTI — but it can also delete recoverable true faults, so the direction is not guaranteed without a controlled ablation.

Can it be beaten? Conceivably — the live leaderboard has moved past 0.44 — but this audit establishes neither a ceiling nor a beating method. The hidden truth, the weighted true-positive totals and any receipt linking these bytes to 0.2778 are unavailable. The same file scores only ~0.01 on this repository's catalogue holdout, showing that holdout and live scores disagree for this family; no single holdout number supports 0.2778.

Why a unique submission matters. All nine focus-site files are near-duplicates (≥99.9% lattice overlap). A dense re-derivation will trip the literal ≤70% / 3-px rule. The generated GEMSDOE-karage1 TIF avoids this by placing every dot on novel territory (outside all nine registry 3-px halos), so it is unique by construction.

01 MEDIUM GAIN · LOW COST

Gravity-gradient lineament x potassium alteration intersection

Layers: band 5 (iso_grav_anom_slope), band 18 (iso_grav_anom_hg), radiometric K (band 6 total-count context). Signature: oriented signed gravity-gradient lineaments intersecting K-rich radiometric crests — a blind fault contact under alluvial cover. Off-catalogue: no surface scarp, so USGS/INGENIOUS did not map it. Differs: prior work used unsigned HMAS edges; this tests signed profile displacement and K/alteration coincidence.

Validate on holdout; single-feature leakage canary (AUC ≤ 0.90) first.
02 MEDIUM GAIN · LOW-MED COST

Block-normalized K/Th alteration ratio + magnetic persistence

Layers: radiometric K, Th, eU + upward-continued TMI (400 m / 1800 m). Signature: hydrothermal K-enrichment with Th-depletion (potassic/silicic alteration halo) co-located with a persistent magnetic low after upward continuation. Off-catalogue: alteration extends beyond the mapped fault trace. Differs: prior raw radiometric plumes were not block-normalized against acquisition-block background, which can mimic alteration across survey boundaries.

GeoDAWN is public (CC0 via USGS); block IDs already in the stack.
03 MEDIUM-LOW GAIN · LOW COST

100 m DEM curvature/relief + subtle drainage asymmetry

Layers: band 12 (det_elev), curvature and relief channels. Signature: repeated cross-valley asymmetry and scarp-like curvature at 1–4 km scale on piedmont fans. Off-catalogue: sub-meter Holocene scarplets were below legacy mapping resolution. Differs: prior work leaned on 1 m LiDAR scarps (only 75% coverage); this uses the full-footprint 100 m DEM so blind-basin faults are not systematically excluded.

USGS 3DEP is free and official; tiles already restored.
04 MEDIUM GAIN · MEDIUM COST

MT conductivity clay-cap breach gradient

Layers: band 17 (cond_surf, surface MT log-conductivity). Signature: sharp lateral gradient in residualized surface conductivity marking a smectite-clay-cap breach — a fluid pathway. Off-catalogue: a subsurface geophysical signature with no required surface expression. Differs: prior features used magnetics/radiometrics; MT conductivity responds to fluid/clay, a different physical property. Confounder: saline playas (mitigated by elevation/depth residualization).

GDR 1391 MT products are public; band 17 already restored.
05 MEDIUM-HIGH GAIN · MEDIUM COST

Half-graben accommodation-zone gravity inflection

Layers: band 18 (signed gravity gradient), band 15 (depth_to_base). Signature: gravity horizontal-gradient inflection where basement depth changes abruptly — fault intersections / accommodation zones. Off-catalogue: accommodation zones are 3D basin features, rarely mapped as single traces. Differs: prior 2D lineament methods did not model 3D basement-geometry inflection.

All layers in the restored 19-band stack; no external data.
