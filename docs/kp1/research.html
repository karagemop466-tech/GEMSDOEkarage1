1 Thermo-tectonic conduit alignment (this session's lane) TMI upward-continued 150 m gradient, tilt derivative, isostatic gravity gradient, basement step, 1 m LiDAR scarp metrics, spring and vent density, Th/K and U/K alteration ratios, strain rate and second invariant. Hydrothermal fluid paths follow permeable fault damage zones; K-metasomtism halos brighten the damage zone in radiometrics even where no scarp survives. Prior lanes used the catalogue anatomy or a single potential field; this one requires a geometric-mean consensus across five independent fields and forbids the catalogue corridor. Buried pluton margins and caldera rings (round, not linear; multi-field but no scarp and no spring alignment).tested (3 prior experiments)inconclusive proxy contrast; projection 0.2789 is not a score
2 Along-strike continuation of mapped traces Catalogue traces as seeds; curvature, step-over geometry and slip-rate gradient extrapolated along strike; Landsat/SRTM drainage offsets as independent confirmation. Compilations end where a mapper stopped, not where a fault ends. Continuations are the single most common way a fault is "new" to a database. B2 and its lineage already did a h33 step-over and scored 0.2778; this would be a systematic census rather than one hand-picked pattern. Fluvial terrace edges misread as fault scarps; requires an independent drainage-offset test. 1 experimentmedium — the lineage suggests real but small gains
3 Deep-crustal upward continuation series Magnetic and gravity fields continued upward in 100 m steps to 2 km; the depth at which a lineament disappears is a proxy for its depth extent; faults that reach the seismogenic layer are the ones that matter. A shallow-only lineament is usually a landslide or a lithological boundary; a lineament that persists to 2 km is a structure. No lane has ever used the continuation series as a filter; prior lanes used a single upward-continued level. Regional metamorphic fabric, which persists to great depth but is broad and parallel. 2 experimentsmedium–high, and cheap in data terms
4 Spring–lineament intersection census Every mapped spring and water well as a point source; the lineament that passes within 300 m of the largest number of springs is a conduit; intersect with the multi-field edge consensus. Springs sit on permeability, and permeability in this terrain is fault-controlled. A structure that is wet is a structure. Springs were used as a density field before; they have not been used as a per-lineament likelihood test. Alluvial contact springs along basin margins — the dominant mimic in this basin. 1 experimentlow–medium
5 Differential LiDAR scarp freshness 1 m LiDAR hillshade and roughness decomposed into scarp-height and scarp-degradation classes; fresh scarps are HQ faults, degraded ones are pre-Quaternary. The catalogue is biased toward what is visible on aerial photography; a fresh scarp under vegetation may never have been photographed. Prior lanes used LiDAR as a flat density term; this uses the degradation class as the label proxy.Gravity-driven landslides and fluvial cut banks on steep slopes. 1–2 experimentslow — the LiDAR product is already in the candidate

What must happen before another candidate can be considered

  1. Do not seek or infer a submission receipt for this research-only artifact: it was not selected, its proxy improvement interval includes zero, and no upload is authorized.
  2. Before any new experiment, resolve the input and writer provenance against official competition files and pre-register an off-catalogue, spatially blocked protocol with leakage canaries and matched baselines. The KP1 session has already used three experiments.
  3. Do not treat the catalogue hide-and-recover score as a leaderboard proxy: it ranks the nine known scores in reverse order. Use an independent fault inventory only for qualified within-instrument comparisons, not as a prediction of organizer score.
  4. Keep selection separate. A future selector must examine a candidate only after scientific, format, and complete-registry gates pass, and must bind any decision to the saved raster SHA-256.
