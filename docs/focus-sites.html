GEMSDOE32 B2 · EVIDENCE-LIMITED EXPLANATION

Why might B2 have ranked highest in the supplied nine-site list?

The value 0.2778 is USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED. No organizer submission-page receipt here binds it to a TIFF hash. This is a mechanism hypothesis, not a causal finding.

Local pinned-mirror measurements show B2 is a strict subset of the GEMSDOE25/GEMSDOE30 parent: 6,436 dots were removed, none added; the removed dots lie within 2 pixels of the mirrored known-fault raster. These measurements describe third-party mirror bytes, not organizer-authenticated scoring inputs.

A plausible explanation is a precision–recall tradeoff under the official distance-weighted Tversky metric (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel): a prediction can add false-positive mass while earning little marginal recall when nearby truth is already covered. Removing such dots could help if the lost coverage is smaller than the false-positive cost. If those dots cover real withheld faults, removal could hurt. The score difference alone cannot distinguish these explanations, tuning effects, or a file/receipt mismatch.

Prior-session proxy result: HOLDOUT-DTI 0.004729 (evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2, 45,260 withheld positives, 95% CI [0.004337, 0.005163]). This catalogue-segment hide-and-recover result is not calibrated to hidden off-catalogue faults and is not a leaderboard estimate.

No higher organizer score is established, and this review did not generate or test a new TIFF. Read the current evidence and limits and the detailed B2 analysis. Official metric/source links are listed in those audit records; they were not freshly fetched in this review.
