B2 CAVEAT

A geometry clue, not a score explanation.

On the pinned peer rasters, GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 share the same 44,090-dot mask. GEMSDOE32 B2 has 37,654 dots: the parent mask minus 6,436 dots within 2 px of the mirrored catalogue, with no dots added. GEMSDOE36 is a strict superset with 1,200 additional dots. The reported 0.2778 for GEMSDOE32 is USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED; no artifact-linked organizer receipt ties it to the raster hash.

Under the official distance-weighted Tversky metric, removing redundant prediction mass could reduce false-positive cost, but could also remove withheld-fault coverage. These mask relationships are not a controlled ablation and do not establish why the reported score differs.

RANKED HYPOTHESES

Four qualitative proposals; none is an assigned lane.

Rank reflects mechanistic plausibility relative to cost only—not a predicted DTI gain. Exact inputs, mimics, differences and data blockers are recorded in the complete hypothesis slate ↗.

01 MEDIUM COST · PROPOSAL

Cross-Potential Dip-Polarity Co-Gradient Step (CP-DPCS)

Compare signed RTP-magnetic phase with isostatic-gravity gradients across continuation scales, conditioned on low relief. A concealed fault could juxtapose magnetic basement and lower-density basin fill under subdued cover.

Non-fault mimics: intrusive contacts, caldera margins, basalt edges, paleochannels and survey seams.
Proposed contest-stack layers are not locally authenticated; related ideas exist, so fleet-wide novelty is unknown. Official contest description ↗
02 MEDIUM–HIGH COST · PROPOSAL

Biharmonic Elastic Relay-Bridge Stress-Transfer Corridor (BERB-STC)

Use fold-visible fault-segment endpoints to estimate tip-pair stress transfer, then test whether conductivity and gravity discontinuities corroborate relay-ramp corridors connecting traces beneath young fans.

Non-fault mimics: induced seismicity, conductive playa brines and coarse strain-grid interpolation bullseyes.
Needs fold-local feature derivation and a shared spatial holdout. Contest data and an assigned lane are absent; not run.
03 MEDIUM–HIGH COST · PROPOSAL

Wavelet-Destriped Potassium-Residual Magnetics Trough (WD-KRMT)

Remove flight-line structure from GeoDAWN potassium/eTh/eU fields, then test a radiometric boundary paired with a magnetic trough that may persist where erosion or burial hides a scarp.

Non-fault mimics: potassic intrusions, weathering, volcanic units, playa evaporites and survey stripes.
USGS GeoDAWN catalog ↗ lists public bundles; catalog access was verified, but no payload, exact local projection or contest-grid overlap was checked.
04 HIGH COST · PROPOSAL

Cross-Fan Drainage-Incision Knickzone Alignment (CF-DIKA)

Require replicated channel deflection or knickzones across at least three adjacent catchments at an independent gravity step. Repeated drainage perturbations may survive where individual scarps are obscured.

Non-fault mimics: Lake Lahontan shoreline benches, fan avulsion, stream piracy and lithologic ledges.
USGS 3DEP products ↗ documents public coverage; no tile, alignment or useful-resolution check was performed.

Queue decision: CP-DPCS is the qualitative first question only.
