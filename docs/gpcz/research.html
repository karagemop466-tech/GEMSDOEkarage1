WHAT THE BYTES SHOW

Why B2 may have worked.

Cross-site binary-mask algebra establishes a collar-ablation ladder on the identical GEMSDOE25/GEMSDOE30 parent mask (44,090 dots): removing 3,891 dots within 1 pixel of the mirrored catalogue yields GEMSDOE31 and GEMSDOE28 h27-4-r1 (40,199 dots, 0.2708 user-reported); removing 6,436 dots within 2 pixels yields GEMSDOE32 B2 (37,654 dots, 0.2778 user-reported). GEMSDOE36 (38,854 dots, 0.2750 user-reported) is an exact superset that adds 1,200 off-catalogue dots.

For the organizer metric, deleting near-catalogue digitization halos helps only if saved false-positive penalty outweighs lost distance-weighted recall. Firsthand inspection of restored training_features.tif found 3,073 raw float32 nodata pixels inside the submission footprint and strictly positive Band 6 tc curvature values, both requiring explicit inference masking.

The reported 0.2778 is an unverified user report. No experiment in the active session establishes a higher leaderboard result. Read the complete forensic & 9-site audit ↗

Buried basement-step (BB-STEP)

Isostatic-gravity and depth-to-basement NMS ridges, sign-invariant alignment, low topographic-slope veto. Targets intra-basin faults the scarp catalogue misses. Max leakage-canary AUC 0.53. Unique vs nine focus sites (12.6%); lattice 98.5%.

Confounders: lithologic contacts, volcanic-flow density edges, basin-fill compaction.
Opposite of H19-5 / GPC-Z (slope is a veto). Fold-local HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0318 [0.0271, 0.0363], evaluator gemsk-hide-recover-v1, n=60,988. Not an organizer score.
01 MEDIUM COST

Signed multiscale offsets

Use RTP magnetics, isostatic gravity and detrended elevation: compare oriented, signed cross-profiles at two scales. Stable offsets may indicate a buried discontinuity without a mapped scarp.

Confounders: lithologic contacts, volcanic flow edges and survey artifacts.
Differs from BB-STEP (unsigned aligned ridges) by testing signed profile displacement; global novelty unverified.
02 MEDIUM COST

Alteration-boundary residuals

Compare local background-normalized K/eTh radiometric boundaries with oriented RTP edges. An altered structure under cover might differ from the catalogue.

Confounders: weathering, potassium-rich intrusions and acquisition blocks.
Tests directional residuals rather than the prior raw radiometric plume. USGS GeoDAWN ↗
03 HIGH COST

Drainage asymmetry

On USGS 3DEP DEMs, test repeated cross-valley asymmetry among adjacent catchments, conditioned on drainage area. A degraded or obscured trace could alter drainage geometry.

Confounders: stream piracy, fan deposition and erosion.
Different from the prior pixel-level scarp score. USGS 3DEP ↗; local official tiles not fetched.
04 HIGH COST

Sampling-aware thermal residuals

Cross-fit INGENIOUS 2 m probe temperatures against terrain and access, then compare residual patterns with gravity boundaries. Concealed flow could remain near an unmapped structure.

Confounders: solar heating, groundwater and access-biased sampling.
