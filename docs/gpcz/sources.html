01 MEDIUM · QUALITATIVE TOP PRIORITY

Cross-Potential Dip-Polarity Co-Gradient Step (CP-DPCS)

Layers / signature: RTP, total-field magnetic horizontal/vertical gradients, isostatic-gravity anomaly and gradients, plus low-slope conditioning. Compare signed magnetic phase with the gravity-gradient direction across multiple upward-continuation heights; target along-strike persistence of their joint relation beneath basin cover.

Mechanism: a concealed normal-fault basement step can juxtapose magnetic basement and lower-density basin fill beneath subdued topography. This differs from an unsigned single-band ridge by testing a signed, scale-consistent paired field relation.

Named non-fault mimics: plutonic/metasedimentary contacts, caldera margins, basalt-flow edges, buried channels and flight-line stitching/seams.

Readiness: only in-stack feature names are proposed; the official contest rasters are login-gated and absent here. The hash-pinned mirror is not organizer-authenticated. No local coverage, fold test, leakage test or holdout result exists.
02 MEDIUM–HIGH · QUALITATIVE

Biharmonic Elastic Relay-Bridge Stress-Transfer Corridor (BERB-STC)

Layers / signature: shear/dilatational strain and second invariant, earthquake/induced-earthquake density, conductivity and gravity gradients; use only visible fault-segment endpoints inside each training fold. Predict relay-ramp corridors and require independent geophysical contrast.

Mechanism: interacting en-echelon fault tips may transfer deformation across relay ramps where individual surface traces fade. Unlike static endpoint cones, this tests fold-local tip-pair coupling and corroborating conductivity/gravity structure before placement.

Named non-fault mimics: mining or geothermal induced seismicity, conductive playa brines, and interpolation bullseyes in coarse strain fields.

Readiness: proposed input names are from the login-gated competition stack. No assigned lane, local feature stack, fold-local graph, or valid paired holdout is available. A related planning idea exists in earlier repository notes, so the concept is not novel within this repository.
03 MEDIUM–HIGH · EXTERNAL DATA

Wavelet-Destriped Potassium-Residual Magnetics Trough (WD-KRMT)

Layers / signature: public GeoDAWN potassium, eTh, eU and magnetic grids plus acquisition block/flight-line metadata. Remove line/flight-level artifacts using blocked robust residualization, then test whether a narrow radiometric boundary coincides with a magnetite-destruction trough.

Mechanism: hydrothermal alteration may produce a localized potassium residual and magnetic low where a surface scarp is absent. This differs from raw K/Th ratio or broad plume rules by controlling for flight lines and testing paired residual structure.

Named non-fault mimics: potassic intrusive rocks, weathering/soil changes, volcanic units, playa evaporites and flight-height/leveling stripes.

Readiness: official USGS GeoDAWN ScienceBase catalog lists downloadable GeoTIFF bundles (Area 1: 43.57 MB; Area 2: 230.54 MB) and documents CC0 rights. Catalog availability is verified; bytes were not fetched, footprint/projection were not checked, and overlap with existing features is unknown. Official USGS DOI ↗
04 HIGH · EXTERNAL DATA / PROCESSING

Cross-Fan Drainage-Incision Knickzone Alignment (CF-DIKA)

Layers / signature: a USGS 3DEP 1/3-arc-second DEM (~10 m), competition elevation/slope and gravity gradient. Test repeated channel-deflection and knickzone alignment across at least three adjacent catchments at a gravity step, conditioned on basin and drainage area.

Mechanism: degraded or low-relief fault throw may perturb several neighboring catchments even where no single scarp is clear. This differs from pixel-level scarp scoring by requiring replicated drainage geometry and independent subsurface corroboration.

Named non-fault mimics: Lake Lahontan shoreline benches, fan avulsion, stream piracy, lithologic ledges and resistant caprock.

Readiness: USGS describes seamless 1/3-arc-second coverage across the conterminous U.S. and products without use restrictions; 1 m products have limited/expanding coverage. A specific tile, local grid alignment, resampling plan and useful resolution have not been checked. Official 3DEP product page ↗
